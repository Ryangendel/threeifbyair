Our namesake

One if by Land, Two if by Sea, Three if by Air

In April 1775, lanterns in the steeple of Boston's Old North Church signaled how British troops were advancing. Those lanterns warned a community of coming danger and saved lives — the same mission our survival community carries forward. We add a third signal, and turn it into how you find us: count the lanterns and you know where you've arrived.

One if by land

At the Gate

Drive or hike in — one lantern marks the gate.

Two if by sea

At the Dock

Boat or paddle in — two lanterns mark the dock.

Three if by air

At the Airstrip

Fly in or land a seaplane — three lanterns mark the field.

"One if by land, two if by sea" was later immortalized in Longfellow's poem Paul Revere's Ride.

The property today

A real, four-season lakefront property — already ours

Not a concept or a rendering. This is Fortitude Ranch Wisconsin, owned and operated today by Fortitude Ranch: a former lakeside resort whose shoreline cabins and RV park earned rave reviews for years, on its own forested peninsula, with buildings, power, water, and food production already in place. If the raise succeeds, Fortitude Ranch sells the property to Three If By Air, which opens it to the public as a resort and restaurant while it continues as a Fortitude Ranch community.

Reach us by land, water, or air

Three ways in — and nearly everywhere else has one

"one if by land"

The Land

A beautiful drive through the forests of north central Wisconsin brings you in on year-round roads, far from big-city noise and crowds. Come by ATV, by snowmobile in winter, or hike in to see the wildlife. Cabins, rooms, and RV sites sit back among the trees.

"two if by sea"

The Dock — six boats + a pontoon

Today the property has two small docks (top). Part of this raise replaces them with one large dock (plan, bottom) and buys a party pontoon boat for guest rental. Other resorts and vacation homes share the big lake, so guests can paddle over for a boxed lunch, a cold drink, or dinner.

  • Three boats tie up per side along a single main pier
  • A T-head at the end berths the party pontoon boat
  • Meets Wisconsin permit-exempt limits: pier ≤ 6 ft wide, loading platform under 200 sq ft
  • Heavy pilings, cross-braced, anchored to a reinforced shore landing
  • Paddle about 15 minutes to the restaurant; floatplanes can taxi to the T-head
"three if by air"

The Airfield

Fortitude Ranch has built airfields at other locations. Here we will have a private airstrip right on the property — pilots can fly in for breakfast, a weekend, or a long stay.

  • 1,700 ft long, 100 ft wide, laid out across the peninsula
  • Aircraft needing more distance can wait for favorable winds
  • The cleared strip doubles as a defensive line on the only land approach
  • In a collapse, private planes will be able to land here for refuge
  • Fortitude Ranch members can vacation — and shelter — at any location in the network, and other sites also have nearby airports or on-property strips
