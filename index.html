Our Story

Three lanterns on the shore

The old signal was simple — one if by land, two if by sea. We light a third: a welcome home by the water. 3 If By Air sits on the Wisconsin lakeshore where a restaurant, a lakefront resort, and a homestead community share one long dock and one big table.

Our kitchen cooks the way the seasons ask us to — Midwestern at heart, sourced from the farms, creameries, and waters that surround us. Come as you are, stay as long as you like, and watch the lanterns come up as the sun goes down.
